Our Story

Elevated.
Intentional.
Connected.

Nestled above Cayuga Lake in Union Springs, New York, Sulla Collina was created to feel deeply connected to the landscape around it — walking distance to local wineries, surrounded by some of the Finger Lakes' most breathtaking scenery.

The retreat is a place where time moves differently. Where mornings begin slowly over the lake and evenings end with one of the most beautiful sunsets you have ever seen.

What began as raw land and a vision is becoming something truly special — and you can be among the very first to experience it.

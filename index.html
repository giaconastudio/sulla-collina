Our Story

Elevated.
Intentional.
Connected.

What began as raw land and a vision is becoming a thoughtfully designed escape nestled above Cayuga Lake — one of the most breathtaking vantage points in the entire Finger Lakes region.

Walking distance to local wineries and surrounded by the region's most beautiful scenery, Sulla Collina was created to feel elevated, intentional, and deeply connected to the landscape around it.

At night, unwind under endless stars, sip wine by the fire, and take in one of the most beautiful sunsets you will ever see overlooking Cayuga Lake.

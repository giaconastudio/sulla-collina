Our Story

Elevated.
Intentional.
Connected.

Nestled above Cayuga Lake in the heart of the Finger Lakes, Sulla Collina was created to feel deeply connected to the landscape around it — walking distance to local wineries, surrounded by some of the region's most beautiful scenery.

The retreat is a place where time moves differently. Where mornings begin slowly and evenings end with the most breathtaking sunsets you have ever seen over Cayuga Lake.

What began as raw land and a vision is becoming something truly special — and you can be among the first to experience it.
